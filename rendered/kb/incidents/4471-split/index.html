How to tell them apart

  • Parser: day and month swap. Every day-first tenant, every channel except the booking-v3 form.
  • Hint: the same swap, but only through the booking-v3 form (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type month first, and the form reads day first. Fixing the parser does not fix this. The hint text does.
  • Shift: the date moves by one day. A day/month swap can never do that. Look for weekdays one apart, “before I booked it”, or two cities seeing different days. The desk listed these in its notes and then filed them under parser anyway.

What went wrong in triage

  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It is wrong for many reports in every problem. Use the tenant and the channel (#lib/kb/problems), not the tag.
  • The export repeats some reports. Count by id.
